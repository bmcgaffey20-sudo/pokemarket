Slab Grade Privacy Policy

Updated September 27, 2026. Slab Grade is the card marketplace previously named PokeMarket. Existing app installations and service addresses may still use PokeMarket.

Privacy contact: bmcgaffey20@gmail.com. This policy covers the marketplace app and its backend services.

Information used by the service

We process your email, display name, password hash, account identifiers, membership dates, account restrictions, card listings and photos, AI scan results, messages and attachments, feedback, order details, shipping addresses, tracking, payment references, and notification device tokens. Service logs may contain IP addresses, request paths and technical errors. Camera access is used to capture card photos; notification permission is used for alerts.

Why we process it

We use information to operate accounts, publish listings, analyze cards, facilitate purchases and returns, send service messages, provide support, prevent abuse and maintain financial records. Listing photos, seller display names, public profiles and feedback can be seen by other users. Shipping details are shared with the relevant transaction participant when needed to fulfill an order or return. Direct messages are available to the conversation participants and service operators as needed for support or abuse investigation.

Service providers

The service uses Render for hosting, the configured PostgreSQL provider for database storage, Cloudflare R2 for photos and attachments, Google Gemini for AI analysis of selected card photos, TCGdex for card-reference lookups, Stripe for payments and seller onboarding, Mailjet for email and Firebase Cloud Messaging for push notifications. Stripe collects payment details directly; our backend stores payment references rather than full card numbers. Provider retention, processing locations and AI-data handling depend on their terms and your service configuration. Do not include personal documents, faces or sensitive information in card scans. AI analysis is an estimate, not guaranteed authentication or professional grading.

Retention and deletion

You can request deletion in Account → Privacy & account deletion or through our web deletion page. We verify your password. New marketplace activity stops when deletion is requested. If an order, payout, refund, dispute, checkout or scan remains unresolved, final deletion waits for its resolution; you can still access existing orders and contact support. Once eligible, we remove identifying profile fields, listing content and AI results, conversations and attachments, feedback and device tokens. Cloud-file deletion is queued and retried if a provider is unavailable. Previously issued photo links can remain valid until they expire or the object is deleted.

Shipping addresses are removed from settled orders after the configured retention window (30 days by default after completion or the last update), or during eligible account deletion. Addresses needed for unresolved orders are retained until resolution. We preserve pseudonymous financial records, order identifiers, amounts, fees, payout/refund status and payment references for accounting, disputes and fraud prevention. This release does not automatically expire the financial ledger; contact us with retention or deletion questions. These records are restricted to administrators. Weekly and monthly financial reports exclude names, email addresses, shipping addresses and message content. Copies already delivered to an administrator's mailbox require separate handling. We retain limited moderation-event metadata; free-text reasons are removed on final account deletion.

Provider backups and logs may remain under the provider's retention schedule; deletion from active storage does not instantly remove every backup or previously downloaded copy. Other devices may retain local photos until their app data is cleared. A deletion through the app clears this account's saved cards on that device. Contact us if an outstanding transaction is preventing deletion.

Your choices and security

You can edit your account information where supported, revoke camera or notification access in Android settings, and contact us to request access, correction or deletion. We use authenticated access and restricted administrative endpoints, but no system can promise absolute security. We do not sell personal information or use advertising SDKs in this release. If our practices change, we will update this page. Contact us before using the marketplace if you cannot legally enter its transactions; this service is not directed at children under 13.
