# Backend 2.18.1 — two required photos

Front and back are now the only required scan and publication photos. Optional
defect close-ups (up to five) and older side-angle uploads remain supported.
Duplicate labels, missing front/back, and unknown labels are rejected.
Direct uploads and multipart uploads accept the two-photo workflow.

Deploy before Android 0.21.1. Health reports 2.18.1-two-photo.
No database migration or new environment variables are needed.
