# Sell Instantly
Backend 2.27.0-instant-sales; Android 0.30.0-instant-sales, versionCode140.

Private owner view of published listings: Sell Instantly. Graded cards: Request Review.
Final rules: Mint/Near Mint 29% of AI estimate; Lightly Played 24%; worse conditions declined unless estimated value is strictly over $500, then review. Visible structural damage/major bend, crease, stain, warp, dent, tear, water damage, peeling or indentation overrides Mint/LP and declines automatic offers; value over $500 permits review. Graded cards always require review and never receive an automatic offer. A graded review can still be submitted if AI pricing is unavailable. Values are USD; offer cents are rounded down. Insufficient AI pricing confidence creates no automatic financial offer. Offers below $1 are not issued.

The seller sees only the offer and standard policies, not the AI estimate, rate, confidence or rationale. No marketplace commission applies to instant purchases. Your checkout pays the offered price plus $4.99 shipping, and the seller's eventual transfer includes both. Processor charges still apply to your platform's Stripe account. Public asking prices and regular marketplace fees/minimums remain unchanged. The $19 public-cart minimum does not restrict private instant purchases.

Seller accepts or declines, acknowledging approval is required, payment must succeed before shipping, tracking is required within three business days, and the existing 10-day protection hold after delivery confirmation applies. Seller acceptance does not complete a purchase or release money. Graded/high-value damaged leads can be reviewed and countered in Admin > Sell instantly. A custom counteroffer must be accepted by the seller before approval/payment. Admin can investigate saved card identity, condition, AI analysis and cloud photos; only admin responses contain the estimate and rationale.

Approve & pay opens Stripe checkout for the approving admin's payment method and delivery address. A signed successful-payment webhook claims the listing and triggers the existing sold notification and standard order tracking, returns, holds and payout processing. Another completed purchase wins first; changed or sold listings cause losing checkout payment to follow the existing automatic-refund path. An admin cannot buy their own card. Only one administrator can fund a given lead. No automatic unfunded transfer is created. Existing 10-day settlement worker and delivery confirmation behavior is reused.

AI estimates use stored card identification/condition and model knowledge; no live sold-comparable feed or grounding is configured. They are estimates, not current verified sold prices. Each accepted offer is reviewed before funding. Requests are cached per unchanged listing, with five-minute retry throttling. Initial quotes expire after 24 hours, submitted review/counteroffers after seven days; approved payment links are valid at most 24 hours. The listing remains public until payment succeeds. Report snapshots and instant-lead snapshots remain in the backend for operational investigation.

Deploy all backend files together. New instant_sale_offers table is created on startup. No new environment variable, payment API or external pricing subscription is required. Existing Gemini, Stripe, cloud photos and push configuration is reused. Use Stripe TEST keys for beta; no live transactions were executed during validation. Rebuild Android with JDK17; preserve local.properties and app/google-services.json. Backend: 114 tests passed. Android source reviewed, not compiled here (Android SDK unavailable).
